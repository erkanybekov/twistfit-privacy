  <h2>Data we collect</h2>
  <p>
    None. Twistfit does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal
    data. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no
    third-party tracking tools, and it does not request access to the
    internet or to any other permission on your device.
  </p>

  <h2>Data stored on your device</h2>
  <p>To let you continue where you left off, the game keeps on your device only:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>the state of your current game (the board, the offered pieces, your score);</li>
    <li>your best score;</li>
    <li>your sound on/off setting.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>
    This information never leaves your device through the game and we have no
    access to it. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's data in
    Android settings or by uninstalling the game. If you have turned on
    Android's device backup, Android may include this data in your own Google
    account backup; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account
    settings, not by us.
  </p>

  <h2>Children</h2>
  <p>
    Twistfit is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no data from
    anyone, it collects no data from children.
  </p>
